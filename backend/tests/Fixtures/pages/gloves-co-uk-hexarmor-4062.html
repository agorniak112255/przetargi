How Do the HexArmor Chrome SLT 4062 Gloves Protect Your Hands?

Power plants and electrical facilities carry a risk of an arc flash explosion, so it's imperative that workers protect their hands if the worst comes to fruition. Lab tested for arc flash protection, the HexArmor Chrome SLT 4062 Gloves utilise first-rate cut and puncture protection, as well as an impressive set of EN 407 ratings to ensure your workers remain safe.
