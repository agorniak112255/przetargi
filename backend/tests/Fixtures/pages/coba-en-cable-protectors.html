Why use a Cable Protector?

The use of a cable protector ensures cables are kept well maintained, making sure they are not walked on, pulled or bitten into by any pests. An outdoor cable protector ensures cables and wires are protected from the natural elements such as snow or rain. Trailing wires can act as a hazard particularly in office environments, people are in danger of tripping and damaging wires.

All of our protectors are available in multiple sizes for single or multiple wires. Some of our cable protectors feature a yellow hazard strip for increased visibility, which can be particularly useful in low light outdoor environments. The heavy duty, outdoor cable protectors feature tough edges for enhanced external cable protection.

For more information on how the installation of a cable protector can benefit a specific environment, get in touch with our sales team.
